title: A year of California community college costs $1,288
description: Careers BLS says need a certificate rather than a degree, at California wages, by total pay over ten years. A year in-district is about $1,288 against about $30,408 for four years of in-state tuition at a CSU campus, or $58,240 at a UC campus. Tuition and fees on both sides. These programmes run from a few weeks to two years, so the total depends on the programme.
summary: Two certificate careers out-earn the median bachelor's-level one in California, against eight on the two-year chart. The extra year is doing real work.
date: 2026-08-21
source: BLS OEWS California state medians (May 2025) and the BLS Employment Projections occupation matrix, priced with IPEDS 2023 tuition and fees: in-district at public two-year colleges, in-state by university system. Community colleges that award a bachelor's degree are filed by IPEDS as four-year institutions and are excluded from the four-year figures
