title: Transferring to a UC saves about $76,700
description: Two years at a California community college and two at a public university, against four years at the university. At a UC that is about $81,900 against $158,600. At a CSU it is about $45,900 against $86,600. Tuition and housing on both sides, at resident prices.
summary: The same diploma, from the same university, at about half the price. Most of the saving is rent rather than tuition.
date: 2026-08-23
source: IPEDS 2023 tuition and fees, in-district at public two-year colleges and in-state by university system, with cost of attendance from the College Scorecard release of June 2026 (data year 2024), median per system. Every price is what a California resident pays
