title: Two years of California community college costs $2,576
description: Careers BLS says need an associate's degree, at California wages, by total pay over ten years. Two years in-district is about $2,576 against about $30,408 for four years of in-state tuition at a CSU campus, or $58,240 at a UC campus. Tuition and fees on both sides.
summary: Eight two-year careers out-earn the median bachelor's-level one in California. Most are health technology, and the degree alone is not what does it.
date: 2026-08-21
source: BLS OEWS California state medians (May 2025) and the BLS Employment Projections occupation matrix, priced with IPEDS 2023 tuition and fees: in-district at public two-year colleges, in-state by university system. Community colleges that award a bachelor's degree are filed by IPEDS as four-year institutions and are excluded from the four-year figures
