title: Where the work actually is
description: Every occupation the federal wage survey publishes, drawn to the number of people who hold it and shaded by the median wage. 825 occupations and 155.4 million jobs, sized by employment rather than by attention: the picture is a census to scale, and nothing in it is ranked.
summary: 825 occupations, 155.4 million jobs, each block drawn to the number of people who actually do it. List the occupations and the middle one pays well; weight them by how many hold them and the middle American job pays considerably less.
date: 2026-09-13
source: Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, national release May 2025: annual median wage and total employment by detailed occupation. Fields are the 22 SOC major groups. Wages are national and not adjusted for cost of living
