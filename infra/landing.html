Not every path runs through a four-year college

The comparison changes with the life. Each of these is modeled in full, priced from the same federal data as everything else.

🔧 Straight to work

487 of the 836 careers here are entered with no degree at all. They are priced as a real option: no tuition, no loan, and none of the years of pay a degree gives up.

🏫 Community college first

Four ways through: two years then transfer, part time while working, an associate's on its own, or a bachelor's awarded by the community college itself.

📚 Graduate & professional school

Master's and doctoral, plus medicine, dentistry, law, the MBA and five more, with the unpaid school years and residency stipends inside the arithmetic.

🔁 Returning student

Going back at 35 or 49 is measured against the salary you already earn, not against a debt-free eighteen-year-old. Every sentence on the page changes with it.
